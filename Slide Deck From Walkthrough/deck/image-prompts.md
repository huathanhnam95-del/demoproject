# Image prompts — Architecting Flatland

**House style for every prompt (append to each):**
> Documentary photograph, natural available light, muted desaturated warm palette (cream, sand, ink, soft ochre), fine grain, shallow-to-medium depth of field, no text or lettering anywhere in frame, no logos, no on-screen UI, no illustration or 3D render, editorial reportage, shot on 35mm film.

Every image is graded warm-archival and matted by the deck's `.plate` treatment, so keep the source neutral and slightly flat — heavy contrast or saturated color will fight the page.

Filenames matter: save exactly as listed, into `deck/images/`.

---

## 1. `cover.jpg` — 1920 × 1080 (full bleed, cover slide)
An empty seminar room in a language school, late afternoon. Six or seven light wooden desks on castors pushed out of their rows at loose angles, as if people had just rolled them together and left. Chalk-dust light from tall windows on the left. No people. Wide, symmetrical, calm.

## 2. `threshold.jpg` — 960 × 1080 (half bleed, "The Phase 2 threshold")
Looking through an open doorway from a dim cluttered staff room into a bright, orderly workroom beyond. Foreground in shadow, background in clean daylight. Vertical composition, the door frame roughly centred. No people.

## 3. `cabal-room.jpg` — 1920 × 1080 (full bleed, "The Cabal design process")
Four adults of mixed ages and ethnicities crowded around one small table, all leaning toward a single laptop, mid-conversation. Papers, headphones, two coffee cups, a phone lying face up. Shot slightly from above and to the side, faces partly turned away. Working, not posing.

## 4. `playtest.jpg` — 1000 × 780 (matted plate, "The 24-hour playtest rule")
Over-the-shoulder view of a young adult language learner at a laptop wearing over-ear headphones, mouth mid-vowel, concentrating. Two observers seated behind at a distance, out of focus, writing on paper, saying nothing. Their separation from the learner should read clearly.

## 5. `mouth-mechanics.jpg` — 1000 × 780 (matted plate, "One morning, one module")
Tight, respectful close-up of an adult's lower face in profile, mid-articulation, lips stretched in the tense smile of a high front vowel. Soft side light modelling the jaw and cheek. Clinical but human; no dental or medical staging.

## 6. `booth.jpg` — 960 × 1080 (half bleed, "Three filters against synthetic slop")
A modest home recording corner: a condenser microphone on a small stand, closed laptop, foam panel taped to the wall, a phone propped up beside the mic. No people. Vertical, quiet, slightly untidy — improvised, not a professional studio.

## 7. `friday.jpg` — 1920 × 1080 (full bleed, "A sustainable cadence")
An empty office at dusk with the lights off, one desk lamp still on over a closed notebook, chairs pushed in, low warm window light. Nobody there. Reads as deliberately finished for the day, not abandoned.

## 8. `close.jpg` — 1920 × 1080 (full bleed, closing slide)
A single learner and a teacher side by side at a laptop in bright daylight, both looking at the screen, the learner speaking. Shot from behind and to one side so faces are indistinct. Open, hopeful, unstaged.

If you'd rather do fewer, 1, 3, 7 and 8 carry the most weight — cover, the Cabal, the protected Friday, and the close.
