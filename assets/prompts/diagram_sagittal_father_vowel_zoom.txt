Start frame (0s) — 2D clinical side cut, tongue-focused prompt
Create a 2D clinical sagittal cross‑section (side cutaway) illustrating the start posture for the vowel /ɑː/ (General American “father” vowel). Focus primarily on the tongue shape and placement.

Framing / crop
Zoom in extremely close on the oral cavity + oropharynx (from lips/teeth back to the tongue root and upper pharynx).
Exclude the upper nasal cavity, brain, and neck. The frame should be dominated by the mouth and tongue.
Tongue depiction (priority)
Draw the tongue with highly defined contour (top surface, tongue body, tongue root) using thicker outline than other structures.
Use a distinct, saturated highlight color for the tongue (e.g., strong orange/red) while all other structures are muted pastel/gray.
Show correct /ɑː/ onset posture: tongue low and back, dorsum lowered, tongue root slightly retracted, tip relaxed near the lower incisors (not raised).
Emphasize the open pharyngeal/oral space above the tongue appropriate for /ɑː/.
Other articulators (secondary, simplified)
Jaw lowered (open).
Lips neutral/unrounded, slightly parted.
Soft palate/velum raised (closed velopharyngeal port).
Teeth as simple silhouettes.
Style
“Speech pathology textbook” look: flat colors, clean vector lines, high contrast, minimal shading, white background.
No text labels (unless requested), no arrows unless asked.
Negative prompt (if supported)
Full head/neck diagram, brain visible, tiny tongue, equal emphasis on all organs, 3D render, photoreal textures, messy gradients, low contrast, incorrect tongue position (high/front), cartoon style, labels/text.
