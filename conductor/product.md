# Product Definition

## Initial Concept
This project appears to be a multi-component application focused on dictation practice, potentially leveraging AI for cognitive protocols, and integrating with Figma for design or content creation related to the dictation practice.

## Target Audience
The primary target audience for this application is **language learners**.

## Core Functionalities
The dictation practice application is envisioned with the following core functionalities:
-   **Real-time pronunciation feedback:** Providing immediate feedback to users on their pronunciation.
-   **Vocabulary and phrase recognition:** Identifying and working with specific vocabulary and phrases.
-   **Progress tracking and analytics:** Monitoring user progress and providing insights into their learning.
-   **Integration with external content sources:** Allowing users to practice with content from sources like YouTube or news articles.

## Non-Functional Requirements
The following non-functional requirements are considered important for the application:
-   **High accuracy of pronunciation and speech recognition:** Essential for effective language learning.
-   **High availability and reliability:** Ensuring the application is consistently accessible and performs well.
-   **A user-friendly and intuitive interface:** To provide a smooth and engaging learning experience.
-   **Cross-platform support (web, mobile):** To reach a wider audience and provide flexibility in access.

## Figma Integration
The Figma integration is intended for multiple purposes within the project:
-   **Designing UI/UX of the app itself:** Utilizing Figma for the overall user interface and user experience design.
-   **Creating and managing educational content:** Using Figma to develop visual aids and worksheets for learning.
-   **Visualizing user progress and analytics:** Leveraging Figma to present user data and learning analytics in an understandable way.

It is noted that currently, most design is done automatically via agentic coding, and the Figma integration aspects are planned for future use rather than being actively used for current design work.
