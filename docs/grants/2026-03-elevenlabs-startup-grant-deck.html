Problem

Most English learning still leaves learners consuming language instead of producing it.

For learners
  • Passive videos and audio are easy to consume but harder to retain.
  • Students need more frequent output: speak, type, answer, repeat.
For teachers
  • High-quality narrated content is expensive to produce repeatedly.
  • Manual voice production slows lesson creation and consistency.
For the product
  • Without a scalable voice layer, listening and reading practice stay narrow.
  • Engagement drops when content depth cannot keep pace with learner demand.
BEL thesis

Every passive learning step should become an active language production step.

One core pain: not enough output, not enough scalable voice
Product

BEL closes the loop from input to output to feedback to retention.

Core practice
  • Type, Speak, Fill, Watch, Notes, Pronounce, Writing Challenge, Survival
  • Adaptive difficulty and scoring make each session actionable
  • Pronunciation analysis adds high-signal speaking feedback
Retention system
  • Vocabulary Book plus spaced repetition review
  • Weak words move into structured review instead of being lost
  • Reading Journey extends the voice-led content layer
