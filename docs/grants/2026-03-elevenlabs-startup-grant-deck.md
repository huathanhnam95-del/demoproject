# ElevenLabs Startup Grant Deck

Last updated: 2026-03-08

## Slide 1: Cover

Headline:
BEL turns passive English study into active, voice-led practice.

Support points:

- Live AI English learning platform built around real learner output
- Learner-first product with listening, speaking, reading, writing, and retention loops
- ElevenLabs already powers BEL audio content and can unlock the next layer of voice-led learning

Visual:

- `docs/grants/assets/cover-landing.png`

## Slide 2: Problem

Headline:
Most English learning still leaves learners consuming language instead of producing it.

Support points:

- Passive study creates weaker engagement and lower retention
- Learners need frequent output: speak, type, respond, and review
- Teachers and tutors need scalable, high-quality voice content without recording everything manually

Proof source:

- Founder application thesis plus current BEL product positioning

## Slide 3: Product

Headline:
BEL closes the loop from input to output to feedback to retention.

Support points:

- Practice modes include Type, Speak, Fill, Watch, Notes, Pronounce, Writing Challenge, and Survival
- Vocabulary Book and SRS create a repeatable retention layer
- Pronunciation analysis and adaptive difficulty make practice actionable instead of generic
