# ElevenLabs Startup Grant Fact Sheet

Last updated: 2026-03-08

## Company

- Brand: BEL / Better English Learning
- Category: AI-assisted English learning platform
- Status: Live product
- Grant context: Supporting deck for ElevenLabs startup grant application

## Core Thesis

BEL turns passive English study into active language production. Instead of only watching or listening, learners respond, speak, type, review vocabulary, and build retention loops around real content. ElevenLabs is already part of the content stack and can expand BEL's voice-led learning experience.

## Problem

- Many English learners consume content passively but do not produce enough language output.
- Teachers and tutors spend too much time creating or delivering voice content manually.
- Listening and reading practice become less engaging when narration quality is inconsistent or expensive to scale.

## Product Summary

BEL is a learner-first English platform with product depth across:

- Practice modes: Type, Speak, Fill, Watch, Notes, Pronounce, Writing Challenge, Survival
- Vocabulary and retention: Vocabulary Book plus SRS review
- Feedback: pronunciation analysis, scoring, adaptive difficulty
- Expansion layer: CRM, classroom, and student account / LMS capabilities
- New voice-led content direction: Reading Journey and podcast-style student content

## Who It Serves

- Primary wedge: English learners, especially test-driven and output-focused learners
- Current landing emphasis: A2 / PTE-first flow
- Secondary expansion: teachers, tutors, and classroom operations
