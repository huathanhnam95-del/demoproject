## 1. Executive Summary
This document outlines the design concepts and specific prompts for generating 30 Facebook-optimized profile picture variations for the platform "Better English Learning" (BEL). The variations are divided equally among three distinct branding directions: Diverse Creative Iterations, Visual Evolution of the Current Logo, and Professional & Corporate Tech. 

To maintain consistency and utility:
- All variations include the core acronym **BEL** visible.
- All variations omit any characters/mascots.
- All variations are formatted in a square 1:1 aspect ratio, with key visual elements centered and composed to remain legible and eye-catching inside a circular crop on Facebook.
