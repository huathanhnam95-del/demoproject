Why it is calmer

  • The eye stays in one column while you practise. Instruction, recorder, passage and action bar are stacked in a single card. Feedback is the only step that uses two columns, so the whole result fits on one screen.
  • One primary color. Today the actions are red, green, orange and purple. Now each state has one blue button; recording is the only red, shown as a small dot.
  • No duplicated information. The question heading, the order summary, the status line, the step bar and the tips box repeated what the picker, the recorder and Coach already say.
  • The recorder follows the test. A countdown circle becomes a red recording ring with a live waveform, then a green Complete, so the footer timers and the “Listening to you…” line are gone.
  • Next behaves like the test. It is blocked with a “Cannot skip” message during the countdown and asks for confirmation while recording or after you finish.
  • Settings sit next to what they change. Question filters are by the picker. The sample voice is by the sample audio. History is in the More menu.
