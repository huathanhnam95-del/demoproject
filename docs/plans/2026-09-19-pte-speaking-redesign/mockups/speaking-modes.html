The other six Speaking modes on the Read Aloud pattern

Same rules as the approved Read Aloud screen: one mode bar, the PTE question area in a single card, one action bar whose buttons follow the state, helpers next to the main action and hidden while recording, two-column feedback without scrolling, and Previous attempts at the end of the page. Pick a mode, then step through its states or let the live timers run.

  1. 1Audio first, then the recorder. The recorder appears only when the audio has finished, and uses the new countdown, recording and Complete style.
  2. 2Each mode's helpers sit beside its main button: Replay and Shadow, Replay question, Intro video. They hide while you record.
  3. 3Notes stay with the audio in Retell Lecture and Summarize Group Discussion, instead of being a separate step.
  4. 4Next follows the test: “Cannot skip” during the countdown to recording, a confirmation at other times.

Where each current control went

Same across all six

  • Mode bar: back, mode name, the v7 question picker, Filters (only when the mode has filters), More, level chip. The Basic / Advanced / Settings row and the separate question heading are gone.
  • Progress bar at the top of the card replaces the numbered step bar.
  • Action bar at the bottom of the card: one blue primary action per state, and Next at the far right.
  • Feedback uses two columns: what you said on the left, scores and next steps on the right, with a second tab for the mode's extra material.
  • Previous attempts always sit at the end of the page, newest first, with a trend badge.
