Mission

Mission

Our mission is to help learners practice English in a way that is structured, adaptive, and sustainable enough to support daily improvement. BEL is designed to make deliberate practice feel clear, measurable, and worth returning to every day.

Problem

Passive consumption isn't enough.

Most English learners spend too much time listening or reading, but don't produce enough output. Teachers simply cannot manually scale daily speaking and dictation feedback.

One-size-fits-all doesn't work — every learner needs a unique path
Product

What BEL Builds

BEL combines multiple practice modes—dictation, speaking, pronunciation analysis, and spaced repetition—into a seamless daily engine built for fast feedback and measurable growth.

Adaptive Practice

Adjusts difficulty instantly so you stay in the perfect challenge zone.

Actionable Feedback

Word-level acoustic analysis shows you exactly what to fix.

Retention Loops

FSRS spaced repetition tracks your weak words forever.

Impact

Why BEL Matters

Language outcomes depend on repetition, feedback, and retention. BEL unifies these critical elements into a single experience instead of scattering them across disconnected tools.

Team

Team and Execution

BEL is educator-led and execution-oriented. The project is supported by a broader working team of about 10 people, including the founder, and a tutor network of 26 tutors. That combination gives BEL both product-building momentum and direct exposure to how learners and teachers actually work.

Built Around Real Practice

The product design is shaped by how learners train every day, not by generic content libraries.

Live and Evolving

BEL already has a live product surface and continues to expand its adaptive, feedback, and content systems.

Clear Use for Support

Startup support can accelerate AI services, data and learner analytics, backend reliability, content processing workflows, and the infrastructure needed to serve a richer product at scale.

Support Fit

Why BEL Is a Strong Fit for Startup Support

BEL is not only a content product. It is a product that depends on reliable backend systems, user data, adaptive logic, AI-assisted features, and scalable delivery. As the platform grows, the technical needs become more cloud-intensive, not less.

External support would be directly relevant to BEL's roadmap: improving AI-powered feedback and personalization, strengthening data infrastructure around learner progress, supporting content and voice workflows, and giving the product a stronger foundation for growth as more learners use the system.
