About BEL

A live English learning product built around active practice.

BEL is an AI-assisted English learning platform that helps learners move from passive study to active language production. Instead of only consuming English content, learners type, speak, review, and receive feedback through one adaptive practice system.

Mission

Mission

Our mission is to help learners practice English in a way that is structured, adaptive, and sustainable enough to support daily improvement. BEL is designed to make deliberate practice feel clear, measurable, and worth returning to every day.

Problem

Problem

Most English learners spend too much time consuming content passively. They watch, listen, or read, but they do not produce enough language output. At the same time, teachers and tutors cannot scale consistent, high-quality speaking, dictation, and pronunciation practice manually.

One-size-fits-all doesn't work — each learner needs a different path

The result is a common gap: learners often understand more English than they can actively produce, and practice quality becomes inconsistent when it depends on manual preparation.

Product

What BEL Builds

BEL combines multiple practice modes in one platform: dictation, sentence speaking, fill-in-the-blank, watch mode, note-taking, pronunciation analysis, vocabulary review, and spaced repetition. The product is built around active practice, fast feedback, and repeatable retention loops.

Adaptive Practice

The system adjusts difficulty so learners stay in the right challenge zone instead of bouncing between material that is too easy or too hard.

Feedback That Guides Action

Pronunciation analysis, scoring, and structured retries show learners what to fix instead of only giving them another exercise.

Retention Loops

Vocabulary capture and spaced repetition turn missed words and weak skills into a review system designed to improve long-term recall.

Audience

Who We Serve

BEL's primary wedge is output-focused English learners, especially learners preparing for structured exam tasks such as PTE. The product also has a clear expansion path into tutoring, classroom support, and learner operations for teachers and education teams.

Test-driven learners who need daily speaking and writing output.
Learners who want corrective feedback, not only content consumption.
Tutors and education teams who need more scalable guided practice.
Impact

Why BEL Matters

BEL matters because language outcomes depend on repetition, feedback, and retention, and those are some of the hardest parts of learning to scale well. BEL already brings these elements together in one product experience rather than scattering them across disconnected tools.

For startup programs and grant partners, BEL represents a practical opportunity to support a live learning product with a clear learner problem, a working product surface, and a direct path to stronger AI personalization, richer voice-led content, and scalable delivery as usage grows.

Team

Team and Execution

BEL is educator-led and execution-oriented. The project is supported by a broader working team of about 10 people, including the founder, and a tutor network of 26 tutors. That combination gives BEL both product-building momentum and direct exposure to how learners and teachers actually work.

Built Around Real Practice

The product design is shaped by how learners train every day, not by generic content libraries.

Live and Evolving

Support Fit

Why BEL Is a Strong Fit for Startup Support

BEL is not only a content product. It is a product that depends on reliable backend systems, user data, adaptive logic, AI-assisted features, and scalable delivery. As the platform grows, the technical needs become more cloud-intensive, not less.

External support would be directly relevant to BEL's roadmap: improving AI-powered feedback and personalization, strengthening data infrastructure around learner progress, supporting content and voice workflows, and giving the product a stronger foundation for growth as more learners use the system.
