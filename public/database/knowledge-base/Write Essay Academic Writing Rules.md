# Write Essay Academic Writing Rules

These rules apply to all Write Essay sample essays (A2-B1, B2, and C1). They are treated as hard requirements during QA.

## Sentence Starters (Hard)

Do not start a sentence with these conjunctions:

- `And`
- `But`
- `Or`
- `So`
- `Because`

Preferred alternatives:

- `Another reason is ...`
- `One clear advantage is ...`
- `This result supports the view that ...`
- `A practical example is ...`

## Comma Before Clause-Joining Conjunctions (Hard)

When a coordinating conjunction joins two independent clauses, put a comma before the conjunction.

Correct:

- `Many people use cars, and they also rely on buses.`
- `Public transport is often cheaper, but it may be less convenient.`

Incorrect:

- `Many people use cars and they also rely on buses.`

## Conjunctive Adverbs: Semicolon + Comma (Hard)

When `however` or `therefore` connects two clauses, use:

- a semicolon before the word
- a comma after the word

Correct:

- `He is tall; however, he is skinny.`
- `The plan is expensive; therefore, it needs careful budgeting.`

Incorrect:

- `He is tall. However, he is skinny.`
- `The plan is expensive, therefore it needs careful budgeting.`

Practical guidance:

- Prefer topic-driven sentence starts rather than starting a new sentence with `However,` or `Therefore,`.
- If `however` / `therefore` appear, they must follow the exact punctuation pattern: `; however,` / `; therefore,`.
