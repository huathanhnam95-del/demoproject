BEL is a live AI-assisted English learning product that helps learners move from passive study to active language use. This page gives startup programs, grant reviewers, and support partners a clear view of what BEL has built, where it is today, and what outside support would help us do next.

Snapshot

Product Snapshot

BEL is a learner-first product focused on active English practice. It combines practice modes, feedback, vocabulary retention, and adaptive logic in one system so learners can build stronger output habits over time instead of relying on passive exposure alone.

Problem

Problem

Many English learners consume content but do not produce enough language. They may understand what they hear or read, but they do not get enough structured speaking, typing, recall, and correction practice to build confident output.

This problem is hard to solve manually. High-quality guided practice takes time to create, feedback is difficult to deliver consistently, and retention systems are often disconnected from the practice itself.

Today

Product Today

BEL already has a working learner product surface. It includes dictation, speaking, fill-in-the-blank, note-taking, pronunciation review, vocabulary review, spaced repetition, and adaptive difficulty. The platform is built to make active practice repeatable, trackable, and easier to improve over time.

Practice Modes

Multiple ways to train listening, speaking, recall, and written output within one product.

Feedback Systems

Pronunciation analysis, scoring, retries, and adaptive support help learners understand what to improve.

Retention Tools

Vocabulary capture and spaced repetition turn mistakes into a long-term review loop.

Why Now

Why Support Matters Now

BEL is at a stage where support can accelerate a real product rather than fund a concept. The learner problem is clear, the product direction is clear, and the next step is to strengthen the systems that make the platform more useful, more intelligent, and more scalable.

In practical terms, outside support would help BEL move faster on product quality, backend maturity, and AI-assisted capabilities without losing focus on the learner experience.

Planned Use of Support

Backend and data infrastructure

Content and voice workflows

Scalable delivery

Fit

Why BEL Fits Startup Programs

BEL is a strong fit for startup and grant support because it has a clear learner problem, a live product, and a practical technical roadmap. BEL does not need support to discover what it is. It needs support to deepen what already works and scale the systems behind it.

That makes BEL a good candidate for programs that want to support products with real technical use cases, direct user value, and a credible path from support to stronger product capability.

Execution

Team and Execution

BEL is educator-led and execution-oriented. The project is supported by a broader working team of about 10 people, including the founder, and a tutor network of 26 tutors. This gives BEL both product momentum and direct exposure to real learner and teaching needs.

Built from practice reality

The product is shaped by real learning workflows, not abstract edtech positioning.

Live and improving

Support with a clear destination
