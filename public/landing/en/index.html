FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to sign up to try?

No! You can try the demo immediately without an account. Sign up (free) to save your progress and access the full sentence library.

How is pronunciation scored?

We use Praat acoustic analysis to compare your recording against native audio. You get word-level accuracy, stress pattern analysis, and syllable timing—not just a generic "good/bad" score.

What is FSRS?

FSRS (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler) is a modern, research-backed algorithm that schedules your vocabulary reviews at optimal intervals for long-term retention. It's more effective than the older SM-2 algorithm used by most apps.

Which levels is this for?

BEL is designed for A2 to C1 learners. The adaptive difficulty system adjusts to your performance, so you're always practicing at the right level.

Is it really free?

Yes, 100% free. All features, all sentences, no limits. We built this because we wanted a tool that combines dictation, pronunciation feedback, and SRS in one place—and we want it accessible to everyone.

What content is included?

6,600+ sentences from real English sources, including exam-style content (PTE, IELTS difficulty). Sentences range from 5-word phrases to complex structures.
