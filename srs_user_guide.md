# How Your Review Schedule Works

Welcome to your new personalized learning engine! We've updated the system to make sure you learn vocabulary as efficiently as possible. Here is a simple guide on how it works and how to use it.

## 1. Choose Your "Brain Type" (Algorithms)

We now offer two ways to schedule your cards. You can think of them as the "Classic" reliable methods and the "New AI" method.

### 🧠 The Standard Engine (SM-2)

* **Best for:** Most users who want a proven, steady pace.
* **How it works:** It uses a standard formula used by millions of language learners (like Anki).
* **The Vibe:** Consistent. If you get a card right, it shows up later. If you get it wrong, it shows up sooner. It respects your "Hard" and "Easy" ratings strictly.

### 🚀 The Smart AI Engine (FSRS)

* **Best for:** Power users who want to study *less* but remember *more*.
* **How it works:** It uses machine learning to predict exactly when you are about to forget a word.
* **The Vibe:** Efficient. It might show you cards less often than the Standard engine because it knows you remember them well. It adapts to your specific memory strength.

---

## 2. What the Buttons Do

When you flip a card, you tell the system how well you remembered it. Be honest—it helps the algorithm help you!

### 🔴 Again ("I Forgot")

* **When to use:** You completely blanked or got it wrong.
* **What happens:**
  * The card goes back to **School Mode**.
  * You will see it again in **1 minute** and then **10 minutes** to make sure it sticks.
  * *Tip: Don't feel bad! Forgetting is part of learning.*

### 🟠 Hard ("It Was Tough")

* **When to use:** You remembered it, but you had to really think or almost got it wrong.
* **What happens:**
  * The deeper scheduling waits just a little bit longer (e.g., 1.2x longer) than last time.
  * It slows down how fast this card grows, so you see it more often than easy cards.

### 🟢 Good ("I Got It")

* **When to use:** You remembered it with a normal amount of effort.
* **What happens:**
  * This is the standard "Thumps Up". The card moves forward normally.
  * If it's new, you'll see it tomorrow. If it's old, you might see it in a few days or weeks.

### 🔵 Easy ("Piece of Cake")

* **When to use:** You knew the answer instantly without thinking.
* **What happens:**
  * **Turbo Boost!** The system pushes this card far into the future (e.g., 4 days immediately).
  * You won't be bothered by this card for a while.

---

## 3. Summary for Success

* Start with **Standard (SM-2)**.
* Trust the buttons: **Again** resets, **Good** maintains, **Easy** accelerates.
* Review every day for the best results!
