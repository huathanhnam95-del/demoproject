BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Comfort comes first.

  1. Face a soft light. Keep the camera near eye level.
  2. Relax your shoulders. Leave a little room above your head.
  3. Read one thought at a time. Pause; there is no rush.
  4. Hold still for two seconds before and after speaking.
